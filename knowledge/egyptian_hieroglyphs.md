# Egyptian hieroglyphs

## Origins
Hieroglyphic writing appears in Egypt around 3200 BCE. It combines logograms, phonograms (mostly consonants) and determinatives. Cursive derivatives followed: hieratic (priestly, ink on papyrus) and later demotic (from c. 7th century BCE). The last dated hieroglyphic inscription is at Philae, 394 CE.

## Decipherment
The Rosetta Stone (a decree of Ptolemy V, 196 BCE; found 1799) carries the same text in hieroglyphic, demotic and Greek. Thomas Young identified royal names; Jean-François Champollion announced the phonetic decipherment in 1822.

## Reading notes
Text runs in either direction; signs face the beginning of the line. Names of rulers are enclosed in cartouches. Reading direction, register (monumental vs. cursive) and period strongly affect interpretation.

Sources: British Museum, "The Rosetta Stone"; Allen, *Middle Egyptian* (3rd ed.); Britannica "Egyptian hieroglyphic writing".
